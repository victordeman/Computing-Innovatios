Group Projects

The group project is the centerpiece of the "Computing Innovation and New Technologies" course. It provides students with the opportunity to deep-dive into a specific emerging technology and evaluate its potential to disrupt industries or solve societal challenges.

Team Structure & Timeline

  • Teams: 3–4 students per team.
  • Selection: Topics must be submitted for approval by Week 4.
  • Progress Check: A 2-page progress report is due by Week 9.
  • Final Delivery: Reports and presentations occur in Weeks 13 and 14.

Deliverables

1. Analysis Report

A 10–15 page document analyzing the technology's technical feasibility, economic impact, and societal implications.

2. Presentation

A 10-minute pitch followed by a 5-minute Q&A session with the class and instructor.

3. Prototype (Optional)

A high-level mockup or demo using tools like Figma, Python, or various no-code platforms.

Example Projects

Generative AI for Personalized Education

How LLMs can be deployed in low-resource settings to provide adaptive tutoring at scale.

Quantum-Inspired Logistics

Applying quantum annealing concepts to optimize supply chain routes in real-time.

Decentralized Identity Frameworks

Analyzing the feasibility of blockchain-based SSI (Self-Sovereign Identity) for refugee document verification.
