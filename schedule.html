Course Schedule

Week Topic Readings & Notes
Week 1 Introduction to Computing Innovation Course intro, definitions of innovation, historical context.
Reading: The Innovators (Isaacson) Ch. 1
Week 2 Innovation Frameworks Examination of various frameworks used to understand and categorize innovation, including incremental versus radical changes.
Week 3 Emerging Tech Evaluation Methodologies for assessing new technologies: technical feasibility, market readiness, and sustainability.
Week 4 Case Study: Personal Computing Deep dive into the evolution of personal computers and their impact on productivity and communication.
Week 5 Case Study: The Internet Origins and expansion of the global internet, and the architectural decisions that enabled its scale.
Week 6 Disruptive Potential Identifying characteristics of technologies that displace established market leaders and create new value networks.
Week 7 Midterm Review Midterm Exam
Review session covering topics from the first six weeks.
Week 8 Artificial Intelligence Current state and future trajectories of AI, including machine learning and neural networks.
Week 9 Blockchain & Web3 Introduction to decentralized ledger technologies and the concept of Web3.
Week 10 Quantum Computing Principles of quantum mechanics applied to computing and its potential problem-solving capabilities.
Week 11 Ethics in Computing Ethical considerations: data privacy, algorithmic bias, and social responsibility.
Week 12 Legal & Economic Impacts Regulatory landscapes and economic shifts driven by computing innovations.
Week 13 Future Forecasting Techniques for predicting future technology trends and envisioning the next frontiers.
Week 14 Future Trends, Forecasting, and Project Presentations Final student presentations and looking ahead at emerging frontiers.
