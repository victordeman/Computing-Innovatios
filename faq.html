Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a programming-heavy course?

While the focus is on innovation frameworks and impacts, the group project allows for technical prototyping. You should have intermediate programming knowledge, but the majority of the work is analytical.

What kind of prototypes are expected?

Prototypes can range from Figma mockups to Python demos or no-code applications. The goal is to demonstrate the high-level functionality of your proposed innovation.

How are the teams formed for the group project?

Teams are typically formed by students themselves in the first three weeks. We will also have a "mixer" session to help students find teammates with complementary skills.

Are the readings mandatory?

Yes. Reading responses account for a significant portion of your participation grade and are essential for engaging in class discussions.

Can I work on a project idea I already have?

Absolutely, as long as it fits within the scope of "Computing Innovation" and is approved by the instructor.
