Course Overview

Most of the applications and services we interact with today are driven by rapid innovations in computing. This course provides a comprehensive exploration of how these innovations emerge, how they are evaluated, and their broader implications on society and industry.

This class teaches the design, implementation, and analysis techniques required to understand and propose new technology applications. Topics include innovation frameworks, disruptive potential analysis, historical case studies, and the ethical, legal, and economic implications of computing technology.

What You'll Learn

  • Innovation concepts and frameworks
  • Evaluating emerging tech feasibility
  • Case studies of disruptive innovations
  • Prototyping new tech applications
  • Ethical & societal implications

Assessment

  • Participation & Reading (15–20%)
  • Midterm Exam (20%)
  • Group Project (30–35%)
  • Final Exam / Report (25–30%)

Group Project

Students will work in teams of 3–4 to select an emerging technology or innovation idea. You will deliver a comprehensive analysis report, a presentation, and an optional high-level prototype using no-code tools, Python, or Figma.
